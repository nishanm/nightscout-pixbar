BDF font files (`spleen-12x24.bdf`, `spleen-8x16.bdf`, `spleen-6x12.bdf`).
Only the subset of characters a glucose face can display is included.

## ktomy/nightscout-clock

`sources.py` is a port of the Dexcom Share and Nightscout logic from
[ktomy/nightscout-clock](https://github.com/ktomy/nightscout-clock) - the
endpoints, application IDs, two-step login, backfill window and trend
vocabulary all follow `src/BGSourceDexcom.cpp` and `src/BGSourceNightscout.cpp`.
The glucose band colours in `render.py` are that firmware's RGB565 constants
from `src/enums.h`. Check its licence before publishing this repository.

## Ulanzi TC002 protocol

The custom-app HTTP/MQTT protocol this pushes to is documented by Ulanzi at
[UlanziTechnology/Ulanzi-U-Clock-TC002](https://github.com/UlanziTechnology/Ulanzi-U-Clock-TC002).
No vendor code is included here.
